Which specialization fits you best?

Compare university specializations on several criteria and get a ranked recommendation based on a student's profile and preferences.

How it works

  1. Add the students and the specializations to compare.
  2. Define the criteria, their type (maximize or minimize) and their weights.
  3. Enter the evaluations: a score from 1 to 10 for each specialization and criterion.
  4. Open Results to see the scores, the ranking and the recommendation.

Each score is normalized, multiplied by the criterion weight, then summed (Weighted Sum Model). Ties are detected automatically.
